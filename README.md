## Data and archive layers

These repositories preserve source history, broad datasets, or recovered material rather than serving as the shortest route to a theorem:

- [`open-math-frontier`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/open-math-frontier) — 9,926 structured open targets;
- [`frontier-math-target-data`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/frontier-math-target-data) — problem/technique data;
- [`unpublished-math-papers`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/unpublished-math-papers) — public manuscript/source archive;
- [`erdos-campaign-archive`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/erdos-campaign-archive) — original research-run archive;
- [`erdos-ore-findings`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/erdos-ore-findings) — mathematics recovered from the large historical ledger;
- [`mathematics-under-the-wrong-filename`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/mathematics-under-the-wrong-filename) — results recovered from opaque/non-mathematical archive names;
- [`nested-archive-mathematics`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/nested-archive-mathematics) — results recovered from nested archives;
- [`divergent-mirror-mathematics`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/divergent-mirror-mathematics) — results recovered from a divergent source mirror;
- [`erdos-counterexample-queue`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/erdos-counterexample-queue) — explicit counterexamples and corrected finite calculations;
- [`erdos-archive-seam`](https://github.com/jaredwilder/erdos-archive-seam) — cross-archive recovery map.
